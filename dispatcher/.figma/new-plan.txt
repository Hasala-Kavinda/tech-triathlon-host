16:## 2. App foundations
17:
18:| Item | Rule |
19:|---|---|
20:| App type | Single-page React app with client-side routing and local state (no backend; use the sample data in section 8). |
21:| Viewport | Desktop, designed at 1440 × 900. Content max width 1280, 12-column grid, 24 px gutter, 32 px margin. |
22:| Theme | Daylight mode only (dispatcher is a desk role). |
23:| Colours | Semantic tokens from the design system: `bg` #F5F7FB, `surface` #FFFFFF, `border` #D9DDE8, `text-primary` #0B1437, `text-secondary` #5B6480, `action` #0047FF, `success` #00C46A (text #047857), `warning` #FFC300 (text #6B5000), `critical` #E5484D. |
24:| Type | Headings Poppins 600/700; body Inter; IDs, times, weights and counts in JetBrains Mono with tabular numbers. Sentence case everywhere. |
25:| Shape | Cards 12 px radius, chips 8 px, pills fully rounded, modals 16 px. Soft navy shadows only. |
26:| Buttons | Primary (cobalt fill, white label), Secondary (navy outline), Confirm (emerald fill, navy label — final "done" actions only), Destructive (critical outline), Disabled (navy-100 fill, grey label). Min height 44 px. |
27:| Shop tags | Fresh = emerald-100 / emerald-900 · Style = cobalt-100 / cobalt-900 · Tech = navy-100 / navy-900. |
28:
29:### AI suggestion style (applies across the whole product)
30:Anything the system suggests must look clearly "AI" and always the same way:
31:- **Lightning border:** a 3 px gradient border, `linear-gradient(120deg, #0047FF, #00C46A 55%, #FFC300)` (cobalt → emerald → sunburst), 16 px outer radius, with a soft glow `0 0 24px rgba(0,71,255,.30), 0 0 12px rgba(0,196,106,.25)`. Inner fill is cobalt-50 (#F2F5FF). Build it as an outer div with the gradient and 3 px padding around an inner div with the fill.
32:- **Lightning icon** (cobalt) and the label **"AI suggested"** in cobalt-900 (#00227A).
33:- **Where it is used:** the suggested vehicle card, the suggested order pack banner, the Review pop-up (whole window gets the lightning border), and the Lightning icon on each suggested order row.
34:- **Motion:** on first appear, the glow fades in over 250 ms. An optional slow shimmer may move along the border (one pass, 1.2 s, then stops); disable it for reduced motion.
35:- Once the dispatcher accepts a suggestion, it becomes normal content (plain border, no glow).
36:
37:### App shell (on every page)
38:- **Top bar** (56 px, Navy 900): WayLink logo · depot switch "Galle ▾" · date · "● Synced" · user avatar.
39:- **Side nav** (240 px, white): Home, Schedule, Live, Remarks. The active item uses the cobalt-50 background with a cobalt left bar.
40:- Clicking **Home** goes to `/home`, and clicking **Schedule** goes to `/schedule`. Clicking the logo also goes to `/home`.

44:## 3. Motion system
45:
46:Use these values for every transition. Never animate longer than 300 ms, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by switching to instant cross-fades.
47:
48:| Token | Duration | Easing | Used for |
49:|---|---|---|---|
50:| `hover` | 150 ms | ease-out | Button and row hover, icon hover cards |
51:| `state` | 200 ms | ease-out | Filter on/off, pill toggles, checkbox mark, list re-filter |
52:| `panel` | 250 ms | ease-out | Page slide, panel expand/collapse, modal open |
53:| `exit` | 150 ms | ease-in | Modal close, hover card close, row removal |
54:
55:Transition types:
56:- **Page change** (Home → Schedule, Home → Monitor): new page slides in 24 px from the right and fades in (`panel`). Back navigation slides from the left.
57:- **In-page state change**: cross-fade + height auto-animate (`state`).
58:- **Overlay** (modals): background dims to Navy 900 at 45 % with 8 px backdrop blur; modal scales from 96 % to 100 % and fades in (`panel`). Closing reverses with `exit`.
59:- **Hover card**: fades in and rises 4 px above the icon (`hover`), closes on pointer leave (`exit`).
60:- **List item removed** (Drop): row fades out and collapses its height (`exit`), the list closes the gap (`state`).

109:## 5. Page 1 — Dispatcher home (`/home`)
110:
111:The home page is the dispatcher's **plan for a day**. By default it is **today's plan** (live). Picking another date in the calendar turns it into that day's plan until the dispatcher goes back to today.
112:
113:### Layout (image `01_home_today.png`)
114:- **Title row:** "Dispatcher home" with a cobalt-50 pill **"Today's plan · Sun 27 Sep"**.
115:- **Top row:** three shop-type filter buttons (Waypoint Fresh, Waypoint Tech, Waypoint Style), each with a sunburst count badge showing its active routes.
116:- **Left column (640 px), scheduling first:**
117:  - **Calendar card**, highlighted green (emerald-100 fill, emerald-500 border, soft emerald glow):
118:    - Date tile: day small ("Sun"), date large ("27"), month small ("Sep").
119:    - **Live clock** in JetBrains Mono ("10:42 AM") with a pulsing green "● Live" dot. It updates every minute.
120:    - "Open calendar →" link.
121:    - **Orders due soon**, two tiles: **Due today** (large red number, "2 not scheduled yet" under it) and **Due next 3 days** (count, date range).
122:    - **Week strip**: the next 7 days; each day shows a red count badge when orders are due that day. Today is outlined in cobalt.
123:  - **Schedule orders** button (Primary, full width) with a hint line: "2 orders due today not scheduled".
124:- **Right column:**
125:  - **Active routes** (scrollable): vehicle ID, shop tags, route, shops-covered bar, bell with remark count when remarks exist, chevron.
126:  - **Completed today**: routes finished today with a green border, full green bar and a "Done 13:40" pill. "Show all" expands the list.
127:- The old dark Scheduling status card is **removed**; its numbers now live in the calendar card.
